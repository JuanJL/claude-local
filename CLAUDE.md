## 🎯 The Philosophy

**You are the Engineering Lead.** You don't write code — you manage AI specialists:

```
YOU (Engineering Lead)
        ↓
   "Where should I start?"
        ↓
   🤖 Claude analyzes your Epic
        ↓
   Builds Feature 1 → "Ready for next?"
        ↓
   "Yes!" → Builds Feature 2 → ...
        ↓
   ✅ MVP COMPLETE
```

**Agents do the planning. You make decisions.**

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
